## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Editorial Voice
The design system embodies the utilitarian rigor of a live sports operations desk combined with the typographic dignity of elite European sports journalism. Inspired by tournament centers, authoritative athletic editorial formats, and high-throughput futsal facility control rooms, the interface is athletic, authoritative, and tactfully physical. It rejects generic, floaty software aesthetics—such as pastel blurs, generic illustration sets, and indeterminate gradient cards—in favor of structural hierarchy, deterministic states, and pitchside operational clarity.

### Visual Language & Movement
The design movement is **Tactile Editorial Modernism**:
- **High-contrast, data-dense layouts** built for rapid scanning in glare-heavy outdoor conditions or chaotic pitchside sidelines.
- **Sharp structural 1px boundaries** that delineate fields, court slots, and tabular data matrices cleanly without relying on ambiguous soft drop shadows.
- **Physical press-feedback micro-interactions** that mimic athletic scoreboards, tactile digital stopwatches, and mechanical physical controllers.

### Target Audience & Psychological Intent
Crafted for tournament directors, pitchside referees, venue operators, and competing athletes. The interface communicates immediate stability under pressure. Critical states—such as schedule clashes, red card infractions, and live extra time—demand unambiguous contrast, instant comprehension, and zero layout shift as live numbers tick up.

## Colors

### Palette Strategy
The palette splits cleanly between an authoritative broadcast frame and pitch-level operational signals:
- **Primary (`#0D6E4F` - Pitch Turf Emerald)**: Grounded in tournament grass and synthetic turf. Commands primary actions, valid slot allocations, active indicator tabs, and decisive CTAs.
- **Secondary (`#0B192C` - Floodlight Navy)**: The broadcast masthead anchor. Used for high-elevation stadium scoreboards, top-level navigation, and commanding headers.
- **Tertiary (`#EA580C` - Signal Orange)**: Dedicated strictly to live matches, running match clocks, in-play goal recording, and sudden event alerts.
- **Neutral Canvas & Surfaces (`#F8FAFC` to `#0F172A`)**: The background uses Warm Alabaster Slate (`#F8FAFC`) rather than cold digital white, reducing pitchside ocular strain. Card surfaces sit on clean white (`#FFFFFF`) framed with 1px slate structural borders (`#E2E8F0`).

### Semantic Color Discipline
- **Red Card Danger (`#DC2626` / `#B91C1C`)**: Double-booked courts, critical fixture schedule collisions, disqualifications, and destructive irreversible actions.
- **Relocation Blue (`#1D4ED8` / `#2563EB`)**: Audit logs, court reassignment banners, and administrative operational notices.
- **Pitch Emerald (`#047857`)**: Confirmed published draws, active rosters, and verified court availability.
- **Draft Amber (`#B45309`)**: Uncommitted tournament schedules, provisional rosters, and incomplete score submissions.

## Typography

### Structural Role Breakdown
The design system deploys a strictly disciplined four-font editorial architecture to eliminate data ambiguity:
1. **Headline & Section Anchor (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers clear, modern, and human structural hierarchy for page titles, modal titles, and tournament headers without software clutter.
2. **Display & Category Eyebrows (`Barlow Condensed`)**: Uppercase athletic condensed display used for pitch badges, category overlines, and venue classification kickers.
3. **Core Interface & Prose (`Instrument Sans`)**: Clean, human-centric sans used across form controls, body paragraphs, and match lists. Form input fields are hardcoded to `16px` (`1.0rem`) to eliminate automatic viewport zoom jumps on iOS mobile devices.
4. **Tabular Numerals & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Mandated for all tournament tables, point tallies (`PTS`, `GD`, `W-D-L`), matchday countdowns, kick-off timestamps, and real-time scoreboards. Features constant monospaced glyph widths to prevent horizontal jitter during live matches.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Architecture & Responsive Breakpoints
The layout adopts an operations-first responsive matrix built to transition seamlessly from a pitchside referee phone to dual-monitor venue tournament desks:
- **Mobile (`< 768px`)**: Single-column vertical stream. Global margins are set to `16px` (`margin`). Sticky bottom operation bars and horizontal overflow-scrolling segmented tabs ensure rapid one-thumb switching between court allocations. Minimum touch target height for any actionable control is strictly `44px`.
- **Tablet / Split Desk (`768px – 1024px`)**: Adaptive 6-column grid with `20px` gutters (`gutter-md`). Fixture cards switch to dual-column layouts with persistent court assignment badges.
- **Desktop / Broadcast Masthead (`> 1024px`)**: 12-column structural grid system with `24px` gutters (`gutter-lg`) and fixed-width containers maxing out at `1440px`. Accommodates side-by-side court schedule timetables and persistent filter rails.

### Spacing Philosophy
Spacing distances follow strict deterministic rhythm increments (`4px`, `6px`, `10px`, `16px`, `24px`) derived from practical athletic scoreboards:
- **`space-xs` (0.25rem / 4px)**: Outer padding for segmented control tracks, gaps between tab pills.
- **`space-sm` (0.375rem / 6px)**: Form label bottom margins, badge vertical paddings, small button icon-to-label gaps.
- **`space-md` (0.625rem / 10px)**: Input field vertical interior padding, table cell vertical padding, large button icon gaps.
- **`space-lg` (1rem / 16px)**: Standard card interior padding, field stack vertical separation.
- **`space-xl` (1.5rem / 24px)**: Major section stacking and panel boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

### Flat Tactile Hierarchy
To maintain strict readability under pitchside glare and floodlights, this design system does not use diffused blur drop shadows or frosted glassmorphism. Visual depth is established through tonal framing, high-contrast outlines, and mechanical interactive depressions:

1. **Surface Base Level (`canvas-alabaster` / `#F8FAFC`)**:
   The ground plane. Warm Alabaster Slate canvas that provides warm, low-glare contrast beneath data surfaces.
2. **Surface Container Level (`surface-card` / `#FFFFFF`)**:
   Interactive match panels, venue cards, and fixture schedules sit flat on crisp white, bound by an unambiguous `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
3. **Masthead & High-Impact Deck (`floodlight-navy` / `#0B192C`)**:
   Scoreboards and venue headers adopt an authoritative Floodlight Navy container. Content within this tier uses high-contrast inverted white typography and fluorescent signal badges.
4. **State Feedback & Focus Rings**:
   Focus states avoid blurry halos, applying a sharp `1.5px solid #0D6E4F` inner ring paired with a crisp `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 110, 79, 0.2)` bounding buffer for keyboard and referee tablet navigation.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Geometry
The visual language uses crisp, mechanical geometry with intentional corner radius restraint (`roundedness: 1`):
- **Micro Radii (`0.125rem` – `0.375rem` / `2px` – `6px`)**: Applied to interactive form inputs, select dropdowns, small buttons, and segmented pill tracks. Maintains structural alignment across dense data grids.
- **Standard Control Radii (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Applied to large primary CTA buttons, modal dialog viewports, and score stepper controls.
- **Full Pill Radii (`9999px`)**: Reserved exclusively for semantic tournament tags, live match status badges (`LIVE`, `HALFTIME`, `FINAL`), court indicators (`COURT 3`), and numerical tab count badges.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Mobile Safeguard**: Base font-size is strictly `16px` (`Instrument Sans`) to prevent iOS browser viewport auto-zooming.
- **Dimensions & Box**: Height `42px` desktop, `46px` mobile touch baseline. Padding `10px 14px`. Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Background: `#FFFFFF`.
- **Focus State**: Border switches to `1.5px solid #0D6E4F` with an outer ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 110, 79, 0.2)`.
- **Error / Conflict State**: Border switches to `1.5px solid #DC2626`, accompanied by `#B91C1C` helper validation text.
- **Numeric & Currency Controls**: Renders in `JetBrains Mono` with `tabular-nums` for precise column alignment.
